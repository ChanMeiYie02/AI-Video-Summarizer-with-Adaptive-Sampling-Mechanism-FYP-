# Final Multimodal Video Summary

The presentation opened by welcoming attendees to Advancing AI 2026, establishing AMD's mission to advance high-performance and AI computing to solve global challenges, while highlighting the profound impact of AI across industries like healthcare. The speaker emphasized the rapid growth of the AI industry, driven by massive increases in token consumption and model training compute. This growth is shifting from model training to inference, with projections indicating that 60% of global AI compute capacity will be used for inference by 2026, necessitating a massive expansion in computational infrastructure.

This shift is driven by the rise of agentic AI—systems capable of reasoning and goal-oriented action—which is creating a new growth vector for server CPUs. To support this demand, the AI accelerator market is expected to reach $1.4 trillion by 2030, with GPUs dominating the market. Consequently, the server market is poised to grow by over 50% to reach $200 billion, driven by the need for AI inference.

AMD addressed this challenge by introducing the AMDA Helios rack, positioned as the industry’s highest-performance AI rack designed for running massive frontier models. This system features key components such as the MI455 GPU, the Venus CPU core, and Data Processing Units (DPUs), which enable connectivity and scaling. The Helios platform utilizes the "Triplet Innovation," combining 12 compute and I/O triplets with 432 GB of memory via 3D chipstack packaging, delivering significantly enhanced performance and bandwidth over competitors. Technical discussions detailed the architecture, cooling solutions, and manufacturing advancements, demonstrating the system's capability to deliver unparalleled performance per watt and scale-out bandwidth.

AMD reinforced its strategic position through deep partnerships, collaborating with entities like OpenAI and leveraging open platforms to build models. This collaboration includes the deployment of massive infrastructure, with plans to deploy MI455 racks and scale compute through the Helios platform. The core strategy centers on co-designing the entire data center—including CPUs, GPUs, networking, power, and cooling—to meet future AI workloads.

Looking forward, the focus shifts to the evolution of AI into a broader, personal context. The speaker highlighted the emergence of Personal AI and Empathic AI, driven by agentic systems that move beyond simple responses to proactive actions in the physical world. To support this, new platforms like Rise and AI Halo are being introduced, built on hardware like Ryzen AI to run massive models locally and efficiently.

To manage the scale and security of these distributed agent systems, a full-stack infrastructure solution called Halo was presented. This platform provides a secure sandbox, intelligent routing, and monitoring capabilities, ensuring robust security and comprehensive oversight of agent behavior and token usage. This allows AI to move closer to employees by running workloads on local devices, transforming the PC into an intelligent node.
