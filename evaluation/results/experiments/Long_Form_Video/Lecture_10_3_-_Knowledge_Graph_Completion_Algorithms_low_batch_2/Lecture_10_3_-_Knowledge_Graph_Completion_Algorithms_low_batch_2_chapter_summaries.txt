The video introduces TransE, a knowledge graph embedding technique. The core idea is to learn embeddings for a triple $(head, relation, tail)$ such that the sum of the head embedding and the relation embedding approximates the tail embedding ($h + r \approx t$).

The objective is to enable translation: given a head entity and a relation, the embedding should translate to the corresponding tail entity. For example, if the head is "Barack Obama" and the relation is "nationality," the resulting vector should point toward "United States."

The proximity between the desired result and the actual tail is measured using a scoring function, such as the Euclidean distance between $(h + r)$ and $t$. TransE aims to learn these specific translation vectors in the embedding space by initializing entities and relations uniformly.
