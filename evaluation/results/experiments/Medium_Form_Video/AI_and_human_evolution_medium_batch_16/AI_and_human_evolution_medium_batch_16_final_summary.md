Crucially, the conversation pivots to the necessity of resolving human trust. The speaker argues that the most critical issue is the global collapse of human trust, which undermines international and trade systems. If humans cannot trust each other, the resulting AI will be aggressive and untrustworthy. Therefore, the urgent priority must be solving this human trust problem before attempting to create benevolent and trustworthy AI.
