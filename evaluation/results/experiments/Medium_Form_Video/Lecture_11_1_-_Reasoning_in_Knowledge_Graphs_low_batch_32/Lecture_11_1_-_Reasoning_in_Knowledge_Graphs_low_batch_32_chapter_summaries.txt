**AI Summary:**
The discussion focuses on solving knowledge graph completion and traversal problems efficiently.

The challenge is that applying knowledge graph completion to all possible edges results in an exponentially expensive computational cost, especially when dealing with long queries, as traversal paths explode exponentially.

To address this, the proposed method is called "predictive queries." The core idea is to formulate path queries as prediction tasks, allowing the system to implicitly account for the incompleteness of the knowledge graph and answer path-based queries without explicitly imputing edges or performing expensive traversals. This approach generalizes the link prediction task, enabling the system to implicitly complete the knowledge graph, making it robust to missing information and noise.
