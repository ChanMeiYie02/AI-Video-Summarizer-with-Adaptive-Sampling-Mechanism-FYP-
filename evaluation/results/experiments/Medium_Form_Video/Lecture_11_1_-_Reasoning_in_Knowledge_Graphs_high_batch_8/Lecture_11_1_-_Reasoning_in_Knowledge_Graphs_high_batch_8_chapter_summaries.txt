The discussion focuses on using knowledge graphs (KGs) to answer complex queries over incomplete and massive knowledge bases.

The system explores two main types of queries:
1. **One-hop queries:** Starting with an entity and traversing a single relation (e.g., finding the adverse event caused by a drug). This is treated as a knowledge graph completion task.
